# UK Online Retail Analysis
### Business Performance & Customer Behaviour | Excel

An exploratory analysis of historical UK online retail transactions, investigating sales performance, product contribution and repeat purchasing behaviour.

---

## Project Overview

The aim of this project was to understand business performance through transaction data.

Rather than just creating charts, I explored the data, investigated patterns and checked whether the findings were reliable.

## Questions I Investigated

- How did monthly revenue change?
- Which products generated the highest revenue?
- Does higher sales volume always mean higher revenue?
- Which products had more repeat buyers?
- What data-quality issues could affect the results?

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- Data cleaning and validation
- Excel formulas
- PivotTables
- Customer and product analysis
- Dashboard development

## Key Findings

- Recorded net revenue: approximately £9.75 million.
- Highest revenue month: November 2011 (£1.46 million).
- Rabbit Night Light generated £34,422.09 in November 2011.
- Higher product quantities did not always translate into higher revenue.
- Repeat purchasing varied across products.

## Data Quality & Limitations

- Missing CustomerIDs and product descriptions were identified.
- Exact duplicate transaction rows were identified.
- Negative quantities may represent returns or adjustments.
- Profit could not be calculated because cost data was unavailable.
- The dataset covers historical transactions from 2010–2011.

## Dataset Source

Dataset: [Online Retail Dataset — Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/vijayuv/onlineretail)

Uploader: Ulrik Thyge Pedersen

The dataset contains historical transactions from a UK-based non-store online retail business, covering December 2010 to December 2011.
